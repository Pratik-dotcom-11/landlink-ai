# 🏛️ LandLink AI

### Intelligent Land Record Digitization & Validation System

> An AI-powered platform for digitizing land records, extracting structured information from documents, validating cadastral data, and supporting human-in-the-loop verification.

---

## 📌 Overview

LandLink AI is an intelligent land-record management and validation platform designed to simplify the processing of land documents.

Traditional land records are often stored as scanned documents, handwritten records, PDFs, or other semi-structured formats. Manually extracting and validating information from these documents can be time-consuming and error-prone.

LandLink AI combines **OCR, AI-powered information extraction, confidence scoring, rule-based validation, document processing, and human verification** into a unified workflow.

The system helps transform unstructured land documents into structured, searchable, and verifiable digital records.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Land record digitization involves several challenges:

- 📄 Large volumes of scanned documents
- 🔍 Difficult-to-search historical records
- ✍️ Handwritten or poorly scanned information
- ❌ Manual data-entry errors
- ⚠️ Duplicate or inconsistent land information
- 🧩 Difficult validation of cadastral entities
- ⏳ Time-consuming verification workflows
- 📊 Lack of centralized visibility into record-processing status

LandLink AI aims to address these challenges through an AI-assisted digitization and validation workflow.
